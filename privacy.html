Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

This policy explains what Faceless YouTube Workshop ("we") collects when you register for the workshop and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Name, email address and phone number — entered by you on the registration page, so we can send the joining link and session updates.
  • Payment records — you send us your UPI UTR/payment reference so we can verify the amount and confirm your seat. We never receive or store your UPI PIN or bank credentials.
  • Basic usage data — this site does not currently use analytics or advertising cookies.

Why we use it

  • To deliver the workshop you paid for, including the joining link and reminders.
  • To answer support requests and process refunds under our refund policy.
  • To meet tax and accounting obligations.

Who we share it with

We use only the services needed to run the workshop: your UPI app or bank for payment, WhatsApp for payment confirmation, email for support and joining details, and Zoom for the live session. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Registration and payment records are kept for up to one year after the workshop, unless a longer period is required for accounting, fraud prevention, or a legal dispute. You can ask us to correct or delete information that we are not required to retain.

Your choices

Write to us to see a copy of your data, correct it, or ask for it to be deleted. We aim to respond within seven working days.

Cookies

This site temporarily stores the name, email, and phone number you entered in your browser session so the confirmation page can greet you by name. Payment credentials and UPI PINs are never stored by this site.

Contact

Email our support team for privacy questions.
