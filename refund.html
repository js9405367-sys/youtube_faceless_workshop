Refund & cancellation policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

Read this before paying. This policy applies to workshop registrations paid by UPI.

Refund window

Refund requests are accepted up to 24 hours before the workshop starts. After that time, the seat is normally non-refundable because it has been reserved for you. Approved refunds are for the amount actually received.

If we cancel or reschedule

If we cancel the session, you get a full refund. If we move it, you can take the new date or ask for a full refund.

If you miss the session

If you do not attend, the seat is treated as used and is not refundable. No recording is included unless the registration page specifically says otherwise.

How to request a refund

  1. Email from the address you registered with.
  2. Include your UPI UTR/payment reference.
  3. We reply within two working days.

How long refunds take

Approved refunds are sent back to the UPI account used for payment. Banks usually take 5–7 working days to show the credit; the exact timing is set by your bank, not by us.

Failed payments

If a payment fails but your bank shows a debit, it is normally a hold that the bank releases on its own within 5–7 working days. Send us the bank reference and we will check it against our payment records.
